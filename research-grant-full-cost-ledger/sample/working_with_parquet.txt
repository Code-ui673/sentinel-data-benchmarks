Reading the D09 sample
======================

d09_sample.parquet is a strict row subset of the full dataset (2 awards,
denormalized expenditure rows joined with award attributes).

Python:
    import pandas as pd
    df = pd.read_parquet('d09_sample.parquet')

R:
    library(arrow); df <- read_parquet('d09_sample.parquet')

Command line (parquet-tools or duckdb):
    duckdb -c "SELECT * FROM 'd09_sample.parquet' LIMIT 20;"

A csv twin (d09_sample.csv) ships alongside for spreadsheet tools.
